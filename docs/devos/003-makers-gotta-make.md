# Makers Gotta Make

> "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."
> Genesis 1:1

## First thing we learn about God.

Not holy. Not just. Not merciful. Those come later.

Verse 1: He creates.

## Made in the image of a Maker.

> "So God created man in his own image."
> Genesis 1:27

First principles. If the only thing we know about God so far is that He makes things, then being made in His image means one thing.

We are made to make.

## Our first job was creative.

> "And brought them unto Adam to see what he would call them."
> Genesis 2:19

God doesn't hand Adam a list of names. He hands Adam the work... and watches.

The Creator waited to see what man would create. Let that sit.

## Model Him.

> "Be ye therefore followers of God, as dear children."
> Ephesians 5:1

"Followers" = imitators. Kids copying dad.

What does dad do? He creates. Even Jesus spent most of His life as a carpenter before He preached a single sermon.

## The Spirit filled a craftsman.

Bezalel. Not a priest. Not a prophet. A maker. God filled him w His Spirit "in all manner of workmanship" (Exodus 31:3), then "put in his heart that he may teach" (Exodus 35:34).

Build. Then teach others to build. That's the pattern.

## The charge.

Now, saying all that to say this.

Worship is bigger than a song on Sunday. When you create, you look like the Creator. That's the highest form of it.

Create.\
Build.\
Inspire.\
Repeat.

:::
