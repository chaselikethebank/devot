# Vision Is a Gift

> "Neither told I any man what my God had put in my heart to do at Jerusalem."
> Nehemiah 2:12

## Nehemiah never says "I had an idea."

He says God put it in his heart.

Ezra says the same. The whole book opens w "the LORD stirred up the spirit of Cyrus." A pagan king funds the temple and Ezra credits God, not the king.

The vision shows up as something given. Every time.

So, my heuristic: **you didn't think it up. You picked it up.**

## Even the wanting is a gift.

Church culture gets weird about ambition. Want too much, must be pride.

> "For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure."
> Philippians 2:13

Both to *will* and to *do*. He's behind the doing AND the wanting.

Ambition isn't the problem. Ambition without attribution is the problem.

## Received, not achieved.

> "Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above."
> James 1:17

> "What hast thou that thou didst not receive?"
> 1 Corinthians 4:7

- The plan: received.
- The ambition: received.
- The vision: received.
- The purpose: received.

Nobody takes credit for a gift. You just say thank you and use it.

Pride says "look what I came up with." Gratitude says "look what I was handed." Same vision. Diff posture.

## It doesn't expire.

> "For the gifts and calling of God are without repentance."
> Romans 11:29

God doesn't take back a gift. The vision you shelved 10 years ago? Still yours.

## The charge.

Now, saying all that to say this.

Stop apologizing for the size of it.\
Stop taking credit for it.\
Receive it.\
Write it plain.\
Work it.

::: tip This week
Write the vision down. Next to it write: *I didn't think this up. I picked it up.*
:::

**What vision were you given?**
